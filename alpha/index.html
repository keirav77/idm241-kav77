IDM241: Alpha Description

Google Drive: Expanding and Collapsing Sidebar Google Drive

Description: After clicking the link, click the blue "Sign In" button, or if you do not have an account press the blue "Get Started" button in the top right corner. After signing in you will see your Google Drive page. On the left side of the page, there is a sidebar with folder options, like

  • My Drive
  • Computers
  • Home

We are focusing on the arrow in that button that expands and collapses

TRIGGERS

  • Hover ON:
  • Hover OFF:
  • Click: Expanded
  • Click: Collapsed

Rules: Collapsed

  • Hover ON: The button around the arrow turns grey
  • Hover OFF: Goes to default
  • Click ON: Dropdown list appears with content inside that folder, arrow flips down, icon shakes

Rules: Expanded

  • Hover ON: Turns Gray
  • Hover OFF: Button goes back to default white state
  • Click ON: Collapses the Menu, hides folder contents

Loops and Modes

Loops:The interaction can be repeated over and over, each time you click the arrow it opens and closes the folder

Modes

Collapsed: Nested files are hidden

Expanded: Nested files are visable and the arrow changes direction to pointing up

Expanding other folders won't close the open ones
